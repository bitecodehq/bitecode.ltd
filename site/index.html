Our work

Three products we build and run ourselves, each with AI at its core. The same work we’ll do for you.

  • Zone

    A self-hosted AI workspace. Coding agents on Claude Code, Codex or local models work through a backlog, and every change they make is checked and recorded.

    Built with
    Rust and Axum, React, Tauri for desktop and mobile, PostgreSQL with pgvector
    Status
    Open source, with paid Team and Enterprise plans
    zone.bitecode.ltd

  • SSHoo

    Terminals, files and AI agents for your servers, on your phone. Hand a server’s logs to Claude or Codex and fix it from anywhere.

    Built with
    Kotlin and Jetpack Compose
    Status
    Live on Google Play
    sshoo.app

  • PlotPals

    Picture books made with a child, starring characters they create. Each page’s story, illustration and narration is generated, and the book can be printed.

    Built with
    Kotlin Multiplatform, Ktor, language, image and voice models
    Status
    In development for iOS, Android and the web
    Coming soon

What we’ll build for you

AI that does a real job, inside software that holds up in production.

  • AI in your product

    Chat, search over your own documents, summaries and data extraction, built into the app your users already have.

  • Agents that do the work

    Agents that use your tools and APIs, with approval steps, spending limits and a record of every action they take.

  • Private AI

    Local models and self-hosted set-ups for data that can’t leave your servers.

  • Generated media

    Image, voice and video pipelines, with the cost of every output measured before you scale.

Around the AI, we build the rest of the product too: web and mobile apps, APIs, databases and the infrastructure to run them.

How a project runs

  1. Tell us what you need

    A few lines by email is enough. Jake replies himself, with questions, not a sales deck.

  2. Agree the first release

    We work out with you what ships first, what it costs and when, and put it in writing.

  3. Build it in your repository

    You see working software every week, and the code is yours throughout.

  4. Launch and look after it

    We hand it over with documentation, or keep running and improving it for you.
