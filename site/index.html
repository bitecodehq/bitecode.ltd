What we’ll build for you

We take a product from first sketch to running in production, or pick up one that needs finishing.

  • AI products and agents

    Chat, search over your own documents, and agents that do real work, running where your data already lives.

  • Web apps and APIs

How a project runs

  1. Tell us what you need

    A few lines by email is enough. We reply with questions, not a sales deck.

  2. Agree the first release

    We work out with you what ships first, what it costs and when, and put it in writing.

  3. Build it in your repository

    You see working software every week, and the code is yours throughout.

  4. Launch and look after it

    We hand it over with documentation, or keep running and improving it for you.
